Former Nippon Yusen Otaru Branch 120th Anniversary Festival

Official Japanese name：旧日本郵船株式会社小樽支店 竣工120周年祭

A free two-day festival marking the 120th anniversary of the Former Nippon Yusen Otaru Branch, a nationally designated Important Cultural Property, with live music, talks, kinkarakami art, food and history programs.

Event summary

September 19 at Canal Park: Meihou High School taiko at 10:00 AM, OUC BRASS at 12:15 PM, Maiko Takahashi and Ungappa at 3:00 PM, and Otaru University of Commerce YOSAKOI Shourakubu at 4:00 PM. In the Sales Room: a kinkarakami talk and demonstration at 11:00 AM, a Nippon Yusen Hokkaido Branch talk at 1:00 PM, and the university piano circle at 2:30 PM. September 20 at Canal Park: taiko at 10:00 AM, YOSAKOI at 12:30 PM, and Takashima Echigo Bon Odori at 4:30 PM. In the Sales Room: kinkarakami at 11:00 AM, the Nippon Yusen talk at 1:00 PM, an architecture and museum talk at 2:30 PM, and the university chamber orchestra at 4:00 PM. Flower displays, photo spots, a Nippon Yusen exhibit, kinkarakami workshops, food and shopping are available on both days. Admission is free.

Source：旧日本郵船株式会社小樽支店 公式ページ・公式Instagram
