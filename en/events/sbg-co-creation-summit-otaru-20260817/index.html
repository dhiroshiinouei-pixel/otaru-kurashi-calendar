SBG Co-Creation Summit in Otaru

Official Japanese name：SBG 共創サミットin小樽

A regional co-creation summit in Otaru featuring a panel on local revitalization and a mixed student-adult workshop. Peatix lists the event as 1:00–4:00 PM JST, while the description says a networking session begins at 4:00 PM; its ending time has not been announced.

About this information

Program: a panel discussion on local revitalization from 1:40 to 2:30 PM with Otaru Mayor 迫俊哉, Kyoto University Professor Emeritus 宗本順三, Hokkaido University economics student 江崎祥史, and 北日本消毒 President 湊亨; a mixed student-adult group workshop from 2:50 to 3:50 PM; and networking from 4:00 PM. Admission is JPY 2,000 for adults, payable in cash on the day, and free for students. Participants receive a half-price admission voucher for Otaru Art Base.
